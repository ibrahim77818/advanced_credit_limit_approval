Why this module

Credit control fails when exposure is only known at month end and anyone can confirm an order. This module makes exposure live and puts the right people in the confirmation path.

In standard Odoo 18

  • The partner credit limit is a single amount that only shows a warning banner on the order; nothing is blocked.
  • There is no automatic follow-up: no customer e-mail, no task for the salesperson.
  • Nothing marks a risky customer visibly on the customer form.
  • Any salesperson can confirm an over-limit order directly.
  • There is no approval chain for sales orders out of the box.
  • Rejected deals have no audit trail or customer notification.

With this module

  • Separate, validated Warning and Blocking amounts, evaluated against live exposure.
  • Every breach e-mails the customer, logs the event and creates a Credit Limit Follow-Up activity.
  • Blocked customers get the Blacklisted tag and a black ribbon; salespeople are alerted when they pick them.
  • Over-limit orders go to Waiting Approval, and confirmed orders cannot be enlarged past the limit.
  • Approvers are defined per Sales Team and must act in sequence, each on their own tier.
  • Rejection cancels the order, records the reason and e-mails the customer.

How it works

Four steps from setup to a controlled confirmation.

1
Enable
Turn on Credit Limit Tracking in Sales settings and pick the blacklist policy.
2
Configure
Add approvers to each Sales Team and set Warning / Blocking amounts on customers.
3
Monitor
Exposure and status update on every confirmation, order change, cancellation, invoice, payment and limit change.
4
Approve
Risky orders wait for each tier; the last approval confirms, a rejection cancels.

Feature 1

One switch, one policy, per company

Enable Credit Limit Tracking (off by default) and choose the Blacklisted Customer Policy: Hard Block prevents sales orders for blacklisted customers, Force Approval allows quotations but always routes their confirmation through approvers. Deduct Draft Payments (off by default) decides whether unposted customer payments free up credit. Auto-lift Blacklist removes the tag once the exposure recovers – only when the module applied it.

Feature 2

Real-time exposure, consolidated on the company

The Credit Control tab shows Posted Receivables, Un-invoiced Confirmed Sales, Draft Customer Payments (when deducted), the resulting Credit Exposure, the amount Available Before Blocking and a Credit Status computed live from the current figures. Limits are validated: no negative amounts, and the warning amount cannot exceed the blocking amount. Orders and invoices of every contact roll up to the commercial entity. On a blocking breach the customer receives the Blacklisted tag and a black ribbon; the chatter keeps the notification e-mails and the breach notes, and the salesperson gets a Credit Limit Follow-Up activity.

Formula: Exposure = open posted receivables + un-invoiced part of confirmed orders (tax included, net of posted invoices and credit notes) − confirmed payments not yet booked (In Process, no journal entry) − draft customer payments (only with Deduct Draft Payments).
Example (demo company deducts draft payments): SR 41,200 + SR 18,650 − SR 2,500 = SR 57,350 > Blocking SR 50,000 ⇒ status Blocked.
Feature 3

Salespeople are warned the moment they pick a blacklisted customer

Selecting a blacklisted customer (or any of its contacts) on a quotation opens a Blacklisted Customer alert and keeps a red banner on the form. With Hard Block the quotation cannot be saved at all.

Feature 4

A strict approval sequence per Sales Team

The Approvers tab of a Sales Team lists users in order; drag to reorder. Each user can appear once, and only internal users with Sales access are accepted.

Feature 5

Waiting Approval instead of a blind confirmation

When the projected exposure (current exposure + this order) exceeds the Blocking amount, or the customer is blacklisted under Force Approval, Confirm moves the order to Waiting Approval. Only the current tier sees Approve and Reject; others get an access error even through the API. Each approval is logged and hands over to the next tier with a Credit Approval activity; the last one confirms the order. The salesperson or a Sales Administrator can Withdraw from Approval.

Sales › Orders › Orders to Approve opens on the current user’s queue.
Feature 6

Reject with a reason – the customer is informed

Reject asks for a Rejection Reason, cancels the order immediately, cancels the remaining tiers, logs the rejection in the chatter and e-mails the customer a cancellation notice including the reason.

Feature 7

Fully translated to Arabic, right-to-left

All labels, messages, activities and the three e-mail templates are translated (ar_001). Styles use logical properties so banners and ribbons mirror correctly, and e-mails switch to RTL for Arabic-speaking customers.

Credit statuses

The displayed status is computed live. Breach actions run on order confirmation, changes to confirmed orders, cancellation, invoice and payment posting, reset to draft, limit changes, a daily scheduled action and the Re-evaluate Credit Status button.

Normal exposure ≤ Warning amount (or no limits set)
Warning exposure > Warning amount ⇒ e-mail, chatter note, follow-up activity, amber ribbon
Blocked exposure > Blocking amount ⇒ warning actions + Blacklisted tag + black ribbon
No noise: actions fire only when the status gets worse. Moving from Warning to Blocked updates the existing follow-up activity instead of creating a second one; an improvement is simply logged.

Built for real-world corner cases

Covered by the code and its automated tests.

Contacts of a company
Limits, exposure and blacklist live on the commercial entity and apply to every contact.
Multi-company
Settings, limits, status and exposure are per company; approval records follow company rules.
Multi-currency
Orders and invoices in foreign currencies are converted to company currency.
Draft invoices and credit notes
Draft invoices stay in un-invoiced sales; posted credit notes linked to an order reopen its un-invoiced amount.
Raising a confirmed order
Adding lines or raising quantities/prices on a confirmed order is refused when the new exposure exceeds the blocking amount or the customer is blacklisted; the extra goes on a new quotation for approval. Down payments and re-invoiced expenses are not blocked.
Unconfirmed payments
Draft payments do not free up credit unless the company enables Deduct Draft Payments, so a draft cannot unblock a customer.
Payments without journal entries
In Odoo 18, a confirmed payment on a method without an outstanding account stays In Process with no entry until bank matching. These payments are shown as Confirmed Payments Not Yet Booked and reduce the exposure, without being counted twice once matched.
No bypass
Waiting orders are locked (header and lines), cannot be cancelled or confirmed directly, and context keys cannot skip the check via RPC.
Approvers who see only their own orders
A record rule gives them access to the orders waiting for them.
Resubmission
Withdrawn or rejected orders can be resubmitted; each attempt is a new approval round with full history.
Batch confirmation
Confirming several orders at once confirms those within limits and sends the others to approval.
Missing data
No customer e-mail is logged in the chatter; no salesperson falls back to the order’s salesperson; a team without approvers blocks with a clear message.
